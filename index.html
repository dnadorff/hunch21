How to play

  • Every card is a question whose answer is a whole number from 0 to 10. The phone hides the real answer until the end.
  • On your turn, read the question, tap your best guess, and the card goes into your hand. Your running total of guesses is shown on your chip.
  • You can stop once you hold at least two cards. When you stop, you are done for the game, and the question you declined passes to the next player.
  • The game ends when everyone has stopped. Cards flip in the order players stopped, showing the real answers.
  • Closest to 21 without going over wins. Ties share the win. If everyone goes over, nobody wins.
  • Series to 3 points: the winner of each game scores 2 points. Everyone else scores 1 point if they stayed at or under 21, and 0 if they went over. First to 3 points takes the series. If several players reach 3 points in the same game and are tied at the top, they play a tiebreaker game among themselves.
  • Hitting exactly 21 in a series wins the whole series on the spot (if two players hit 21 in the same game, it counts as a shared game win instead).
  • Card statistics: the deck leans toward the middle. Answers of 3 to 7 are the most common, and the average card is worth about 5.
